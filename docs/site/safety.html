What the evidence does not establish

Never arm real motors during automated tests. Remove propellers and disconnect motor power before a hardware bench inspection. Propeller removal is not permission to arm. No instruction on this site authorizes flight.

Existing control-path limits

  • Service acknowledgements report request acceptance, not verified altitude or touchdown.
  • A companion timeout cannot protect against a dead companion, blocked processing or power loss.
  • Existing live publishers are not protected by the offline arbitration experiment.
  • A continuous open palm is not a deadman switch.
  • Mode prerequisites and flight-controller failsafes require separate validation.

Evidence ladder

Design
A proposed interface or procedure, not executable proof.
Synthetic test
A named case exercised pure code without sensors or aircraft.
Toy simulation
A model result under limited assumptions, not real dynamics.
Isolated SITL
Requires a recorded environment and physical-controller isolation; not executed by this site.
Controlled hardware
Requires separate authorization and measured conditions; not established here.

Open validation work

Linux/ARM installation, camera compatibility, calibration, actual tag detection, power/thermal behavior, CAD fit/load strength, Quest rendering, live ROS integration and individual legal permissions remain separate human tasks.

Experimental geofence, battery, landing, follow-me, voice and swarm logic must stay offline until the relevant interfaces and safety case are independently reviewed.

Operational and income questions

FAA, campus, privacy, immigration, work and tax requirements depend on actual circumstances and current rules. A student label, educational purpose, platform account or unpaid offer does not itself settle eligibility. Verify with appropriate institutional officials and qualified advisers. This site provides no legal advice.
