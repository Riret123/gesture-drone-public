# R03 E02 - independent transient counterexamples
Same 2 A peak for 1 s versus 10 s at a hypothetical 10 V source consumes 20 J versus 200 J. Peak-only budgeting cannot distinguish them; the energy test does. One second at 2 A plus three seconds at known zero yields 1 A RMS over four seconds, verified by sqrt((4*1+0*3)/4).

Splitting a constant 4 s interval into 1 s and 3 s identical-current segments must preserve duration, peak, RMS and energy. This metamorphic test checks a physical arithmetic invariant rather than restating one branch of the implementation.

These are synthetic examples only, not measured motor waveforms or thermal evidence. Unknown samples cannot be silently treated as idle.
