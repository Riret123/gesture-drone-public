# R04 E02 - synthetic mounting fixtures
A 40 by 30 mm synthetic plate with 4 mm holes, 2 mm edge clearance and 2 mm hole gap places centres at least 4 mm from every plate edge and at least 6 mm apart. The checker independently tests these edge-to-edge relationships.

A centre 2 mm from an edge fails even though the centre itself lies inside the plate. Two centres 5 mm apart fail the specified 6 mm separation; 6 mm passes. Duplicate centres fail. A 3 mm wide plate cannot host the fixture hole/margin and returns a clearance finding.

These dimensions are arithmetic fixtures, not a camera/servo/rover part recommendation. Measure actual hardware before any CAD adaptation.
