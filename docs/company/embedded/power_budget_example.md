# R02 E02 - synthetic budget calculation
Synthetic fixture only: a 5 V load uses 2 A continuous, 3 A peak and 0.8 efficiency. Source-side continuous power is 5*2/0.8 = 12.5 W; peak is 18.75 W. At a hypothetical 10 V minimum source voltage, continuous demand is 1.25 A and peak is 1.875 A. These are arithmetic examples, not ratings of any project hardware.

A pair of hypothetical 10 V motors each drawing 1 A continuous and 5 A peak requires 2 A continuous and 10 A simultaneous peak at an ideal 10 V source. Averaging their peak current or assuming alternate stalls would undercount demand. The test deliberately exceeds a hypothetical 6 A peak limit.

Costs, selected parts, actual efficiencies and derating remain verify. No component is recommended by this example.
