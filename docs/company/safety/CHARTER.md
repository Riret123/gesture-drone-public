# Safety and compliance charter
Owner: simulated safety officer. Independent reviewer: simulated safety red team.

Challenge hazard claims and preserve the distinction between offline code, SITL evidence and real vehicle evidence. Maintain a hazard-to-requirement-to-test chain and human release gates. No role can declare certification, legal eligibility or hardware flight permission from unit tests.

Acceptance: every new policy identifies activation scope, assumptions, proposed limits and failure response. Severity is qualitative unless a reviewed scale exists. Do not manufacture occurrence probabilities or risk priority numbers. Unknown evidence remains open.

KPIs: unmitigated severe hazards, missing verification evidence and stale assumptions. Escalate unsafe integration proposals, continue useful offline work, and record blockers precisely.
