# Market scan: hypotheses, not validated demand

Reviewed 2026-11-09 UTC. This is a qualitative opportunity map for Emir's portfolio. No market size, revenue, price or sales claim is established. Every willingness-to-pay assumption is UNVERIFIED. Unknown monetary amounts are written verify.

| Segment | Possible pain / buyer hypothesis | Existing alternative | Smallest safe experiment | Main obstacle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hobby drone accessories | hobbyist wants camera/marker mounts | generic brackets, DIY CAD, existing ecosystem accessories | inspect a non-actuated parametric mount and fit checklist | measured fit, liability, fulfillment |
| Gesture control | educator wants a natural-interface lesson | keyboard/gamepad and existing gesture demos | offline landmark-to-command lesson with ambiguity examples | novelty may not improve usability |
| Education kits | instructor wants reproducible robotics exercises | established curricula and drone competition platforms | software-only fault/replay lesson | curriculum fit, support time, learner safety |
| Inspection | operator wants traceable logs or report automation | existing autopilot logs and commercial analysis tools | synthetic log analytics report, no flight service | real data access, privacy, liability, domain validation |
| Agriculture | researcher wants repeatable field data workflows | mapping/inspection platforms and manual analysis | synthetic mission quality checks | sensor calibration, seasonal workflow, permissions |
| Student competitions / grants | team wants evidence and documentation | team tooling, university support and program guidance | requirements matrix for one verified eligible program | eligibility, deadlines, institution participation |
| YouTube / Patreon | learner wants clear failure explanations | free official docs and robotics channels | local draft episode and offline tutorial | audience unknown; platform and work eligibility |
| Open-source plus kits | learner wants supported reproducibility | free source and established educational products | documented software bundle before physical kit | support, licensing, product safety, returns |
| Freelance services | lab needs test automation/documentation | internal staff, consultants and general freelancers | local sample audit using synthetic data | authorization, scope, confidentiality, qualification |

Primary-source anchors support the existence of ecosystems and programs, not demand for this project:

- [FAA educational users](https://www.faa.gov/uas/educational_users): educational flight has specific regulatory categories; education is not a universal exemption.
- [MAVLink landing-target protocol](https://mavlink.io/en/services/landing_target.html): a defined technical interface already exists. This repository has not demonstrated its live integration.
- [ArduPilot developer introduction](https://ardupilot.org/dev/docs/learning-ardupilot-introduction.html): established libraries and tools are alternatives to building every component anew.
- [NASA student opportunities](https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/nasa-stem-opportunities-activities/): program availability does not establish individual eligibility or funding.
- [RECF competition selection](https://kb.roboticseducation.org/hc/en-us/articles/41245156637975-Which-RECF-Competition-Do-I-Choose): age, institution and program constraints must be checked per opportunity.
- [YouTube eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en) and [Patreon fees](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-gb/articles/11111747095181-Creator-fees-overview): platform gates and fees exist; current applicable amounts and country rules require verify before a plan.

Recommendation as an inference: investigate offline education, replay analytics and documentation samples first because their preparation can reuse existing code without claiming operational aircraft capability. This is a risk/effort judgment, not a market forecast.
