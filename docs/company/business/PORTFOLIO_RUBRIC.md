# Resume-project ranking rubric

Round 02 response to B-01-05. The twelve-project order is a subjective recommendation for visible aerospace engineering skills; admissions and internship outcomes are UNVERIFIED.

Criteria: a reviewer can reproduce evidence; the student can explain frame/units/failure behavior; scope fits two weeks of available study time; personal contribution can be distinguished; no unsupported flight claim is needed; artifacts show iteration rather than a polished screenshot alone.

Acceptance per project family:

- Policy, mission and swarm: state/input contract, named failure trace, tests and explicit model limits.
- Calibration, battery and hardware: assumptions separated from measurements; units and uncertainty visible; no invented ratings.
- Replay and gesture evaluation: data provenance, privacy, split/schema rules and rejected-input behavior.
- HUD and voice: unknown/stale/ambiguous states demonstrated; no accidental authority to actuate.
- Documentation: safe novice path, verified links, attribution and limitations adjacent to claims.

A project should enter the resume only after the student can demonstrate the cited artifact and explain one failed approach. Otherwise list it as current study or omit it. Avoid numeric improvement claims without a recorded baseline, comparable conditions and actual measurements.

Disposition: outcome implication clarified; skill assessment and actual completion remain for human review.
