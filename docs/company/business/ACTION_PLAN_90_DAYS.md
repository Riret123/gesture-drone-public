# Conditional 90-day action plan

This is a portfolio preparation plan. Income activities remain blocked until specific work/tax/institutional eligibility is verified. Dates are relative to a human-approved start; schedule estimates are assumptions, not promises. No automatic external contact or publication.

| Days | Local output | Validation | Human gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-7 | contribution ledger, runnable baseline, uncertainty list | reproduce portable suite and one failure trace | confirm school workload, status and permitted scope |
| 8-14 | one offline lesson prototype | independent learner follows written instructions | verify whether even unpaid research/outreach is permitted |
| 15-21 | synthetic analytics report | validate schema, units, unknown-data behavior | permissioned sample-data review if available |
| 22-30 | two portfolio case studies | remove unearned claims; explain personal contribution | faculty/mentor review, no assumed endorsement |
| 31-45 | revise highest-value sample from feedback | record issues and before/after evidence | only authorized research or service activity proceeds |
| 46-60 | one bounded offer specification, if legally permitted | scope, acceptance, support, exclusions and cost model | qualified eligibility/contract/tax review |
| 61-75 | one approved small trial, otherwise continue portfolio | measure actual time, support burden and defects | no aircraft operations or payment shortcuts |
| 76-90 | decision memo: continue, pivot or stop | compare evidence to hypotheses; no invented conversion data | human decides next term's priorities |

Weekly cadence: one engineering artifact, one adversarial review, one reproducible demonstration and one workload check. If deadlines conflict with coursework, reduce scope rather than hiding support obligations.

Stop conditions: unclear authorization, requests to bypass safety, sensitive data without permission, flight-critical promises, misleading claims or unsustainable support. A stop preserves the portfolio evidence already produced.

Success at day 90 may be a stronger resume and a rejected business hypothesis. First-dollar timing and revenue remain verify until observed lawful transactions exist.

## Round 02 external-action gate

At days 8-14, learner participation requires prior verification of the research activity and appropriate consent; otherwise do a solo instruction audit. At days 15-21, use synthetic data unless a human has verified permission and privacy. At days 22-30, external review remains human-arranged and eligibility-reviewed; otherwise prepare a local case study. At days 31-90, no outreach, service, offer, sale, sponsorship, application or payment proceeds without specific prior human authorization and qualified eligibility review. The local alternative at every stage is test improvement, source review and portfolio preparation. Unpaid labels do not bypass this gate.
