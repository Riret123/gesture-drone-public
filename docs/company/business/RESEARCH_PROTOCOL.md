# Future customer research protocol

Draft only. No messages are sent and no participants recruited by this run. Verify whether the proposed activity is permitted before outreach; obtain appropriate institutional review if required.

Research question: which existing workflow problem, if any, does an offline lesson or read-only analytics sample solve? Avoid asking "would you buy my cool drone project?" because it invites polite agreement.

Interview prompts for a future authorized human session:

1. Describe the last time you taught or debugged a robotics failure.
2. What tools and materials did you actually use?
3. Which step took effort, and how do you know?
4. What did your existing solution fail to explain?
5. What safety, privacy or procurement constraints matter?
6. Who decides whether a new resource can be used?
7. What evidence would cause you to reject this sample?

Do not request private logs, student records or credentials. Record only consented, de-identified notes; do not put personal contact details in source control. Do not promise performance, legal suitability or an available service.

After a sample walkthrough, measure observed completion and confusion, not only verbal enthusiasm. Separate participant quote, researcher interpretation and next hypothesis. A small qualitative sample does not establish market size or conversion rate.

Acceptance: the protocol can produce a negative finding without redefining success. No outreach proceeds merely because this draft exists.
