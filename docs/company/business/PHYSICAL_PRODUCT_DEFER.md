# Decision: defer physical product commercialization

Round 02 response to B-01-08. Local CAD and disabled rover concepts remain useful portfolio work. They are not reviewed products, flight-qualified parts or ready-to-ship kits.

Reasons to defer: unknown structural loads and fit; component ratings not measured; thermal/power behavior unverified; plausible flight-critical misuse; assembly variability; returns/support; product liability; shipping/import/export obligations; and individual work eligibility. None is resolved by writing a disclaimer.

Allowed preparation in this run: concept CAD, dimension questions, symbolic power budgets, wiring review templates, non-powered inspection plans and failure analysis. No driver enablement, motors, procurement or physical tests are performed.

Before reconsideration, a human must define intended use and exclusions; obtain competent mechanical/electrical review; measure fit/load/thermal behavior under an authorized plan; assess misuse and labeling; verify ownership/licenses; verify lawful activity and product obligations; and establish support/recall capacity. Actual requirements depend on jurisdiction and product facts and need qualified advice.

Forbidden marketing without evidence: crash-proof, failsafe, flight-ready, certified, guaranteed compatible, autonomous landing kit or safe for children. Do not silently convert a prototype into a saleable product because a buyer asks.

Disposition: commercialization deferral documented; engineering and legal qualification remain BLOCKED.
