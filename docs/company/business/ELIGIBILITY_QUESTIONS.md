# Questions for human eligibility review

Not legal advice. Do not infer permission from this checklist. Exact status, tax residency, jurisdiction, institution and proposed activity are unknown. "Non-resident alien student" is not enough to decide work or tax treatment.

Ask the school's designated official and qualified counsel about the specific activity before starting: paid freelancing; unpaid client work; selling files; hardware kits; platform ads; memberships; sponsorships; affiliate links; prizes; grants; university research; ownership of a business; work for an overseas client while physically in a particular country. Do not assume a foreign payer, unpaid label or hobby label resolves eligibility.

Record: who reviewed, date, activity description, location, required authorization, permitted dates/hours, school/IP restrictions, tax questions and conditions for re-review. Store sensitive personal records outside this repository.

Primary guidance checked:

- [USC international office: additional employment](https://ois.usc.edu/employment/additional-forms-of-employment/) discusses entrepreneurship and the need to ensure appropriate authorization. It is institutional guidance, not a ruling for Emir.
- [UCLA current F-1 guidance](https://internationalcenter.ucla.edu/f-1-students/currently-enrolled) identifies prior authorization requirements for off-campus activity. It applies in its own institutional context.
- [USCIS students and employment](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/students-and-exchange-visitors/students-and-employment) is a relevant official starting point, but direct retrieval returned 403 during this run; contents were not independently verified here.
- [FAA educational users](https://www.faa.gov/uas/educational_users) separates operational categories; a student project label alone does not establish flight permission.

No legal conclusion, fee estimate or individual eligibility determination is made. Review current official sources and obtain advice suited to the actual facts.
